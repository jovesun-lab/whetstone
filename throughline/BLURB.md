## Releases-tab summary (one paragraph)

> **Throughline v0.1.0.** Long or multi-agent work drifts two ways — sideways within a session, and into a stale restart between them. Throughline is two linked habits with one shared anchor: a **⭐️ MAIN** goal you can see at a glance (Task Track), and a plain-markdown **handoff** the next agent re-derives against ground truth before trusting (Handoff). Pure-prompt and capability-agnostic — works in Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Cursor, or a local model — with an optional zero-dependency validator. MIT.
